### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science graduate from the National University of Singapore (NUS)** with a strong interest in **AI engineering, backend systems, data-intensive applications, and full-stack product development**.

I enjoy taking projects beyond prototypes by working on architecture, reliability, deployment, performance, maintainability, and iterative product improvements. I'm currently exploring graduate and early-career opportunities in **AI Engineering** and **Backend Engineering**.


---

### 💼 Experience

**Teaching Assistant, National University of Singapore (NUS)**

- **CS2102: Database Systems** - taught tutorials, guided students through relational modelling, SQL, normalization, and database concepts. Reappointed for a later semester to teach three tutorial classes totaling roughly 55 to 60 students.
- **IT5008** - supported graduate-level students and delivered teaching sessions as part of my first TA appointment.

---

### 🚀 What I'm Working On

- **Navable** - a self-hosted personal music platform built around FastAPI, SQLite, Docker, PWA support, playback and queue semantics, search, listening history, recommendations, and cross-device synchronization.
- **Foodie Adventure** - a Next.js + TypeScript + Supabase/PostgreSQL food platform covering recipes, blogs, discovery, content workflows, personalization, CI, and performance improvements.
- **Market Research Lab** - a Python-based daily-frequency market research and backtesting platform using DuckDB, Parquet, Pandas/Polars, and reproducible analytical workflows.
